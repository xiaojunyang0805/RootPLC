# F12.ZONE · `RB_ConveyorZone`

**Family:** F12 material handling · **Level:** L3-ready (one per conveyor
zone) · **Library:** RootBlocks 0.1.0 · **Conventions:** spec/15-blocks.md

## Job

Controls one zone of a zero-pressure accumulation conveyor: parts never
push against each other. Each zone has a photo eye at its end and its own
motor (through `RB_Motor` or `RB_Drive`); chaining zones gives a conveyor
of any length.

## Interface

| Name | Direction | Type | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Enable` | in | BOOL | FALSE | the line runs |
| `PartAtEnd` | in | BOOL | FALSE | photo eye at the zone end |
| `DownstreamFree` | in | BOOL | FALSE | the next zone's `Free` (TRUE for the last zone when the outfeed can take a part) |
| `UpstreamFeeding` | in | BOOL | FALSE | the previous zone's `Feeding` |
| `SleepTime` | in | TIME | T#0s | empty and not fed this long: the zone stops; T#0s: never |
| `JamTime` | in | TIME | T#5s | a part handed on must leave the photo eye within this; T#0s: no supervision |
| `Reset` | in | BOOL | FALSE | rising edge clears a jam |
| `Run` | out | BOOL | | zone motor |
| `Occupied` / `Free` | out | BOOL | | a part is in the zone / the zone can take a part |
| `Feeding` | out | BOOL | | handing a part to the next zone |
| `Count` | out | UDINT | | parts handed on |
| `Error` / `ErrorID` | out | BOOL / UDINT | | jam ([blocks.fault]) |

Inputs keep their last value between calls (IEC 61131-3 function-block
inputs), so pass every input that changes, in every call.

## Behaviour rules

1. **[F12.zone.accumulate]** With a part at the end and the next zone not
   free, the zone stops; when the next zone is free it runs and hands the
   part on (`Feeding`).
2. **[F12.zone.empty]** An empty zone runs to bring parts forward, unless
   it has been empty and not fed for `SleepTime` (energy saving); a feeding
   upstream zone wakes it.
3. **[F12.zone.jam]** A part being handed on that stays at the photo eye
   for `JamTime` is fault 1: the zone stops until a rising edge of `Reset`.
4. **[F12.zone.count]** `Count` counts parts that left the photo eye while
   the zone ran (a part removed by hand while stopped is not counted).
5. **[F12.zone.occupied]** The zone is occupied from the moment the
   previous zone has handed a part over (its `Feeding` ends) or the eye sees
   a part, until the part has left the eye; only then is it `Free`.
6. **[F12.zone.enable]** Without `Enable` the zone is stopped and not
   free.

## Faults

| ErrorID | Meaning | Cleared by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | jam at the zone end | `Reset` |

## Combines with

`RB_Motor` / `RB_Drive` on `Run`, `RB_Debounce` on the photo eye, chained
zones (`Free` → `DownstreamFree`, `Feeding` → `UpstreamFeeding`),
`RB_MachineState` (`Enable` from Execute), F08 alarms on `Error`.

## Not covered

Zones with two sensors (entry and end), singulation, merging (`RB_Merge`),
diverting (`RB_Diverter`), speed-controlled transfer.
